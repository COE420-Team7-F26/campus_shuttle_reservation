1. Project Objective
The main purpose of our project is to help students track and reserve seats on campus shuttles. It aims to address the problems of unpredictability as most students are unaware of when the shuttle would arrive and if it would have any seats when it does so, which can result in longer wait times in the hot and humid weather.

2. Target Users
Our system is designed for three main users: students who ride the campus shuttles, drivers that operate those shuttles, and also a transport administrator that manages booking-related issues and account access.

3. In-Scope Features
The functionalities vary depending on the type of users. Students would have the features of seat booking, viewing real-time occupancy, seat cancellation, and they will be sent notifications about shuttle delays. The shuttle drivers would have the features of reporting status updates (such as delays) and marking their current shuttle location. Both students and drivers would be able to flag the other as “no-show”. The transport administrator can review no-show reports and either suspend the account or dismiss the report.

4. Out-of-Scope Features
Out of scope features include allowing students to import their schedules and class location so that the system automatically reserves shuttles that are close to them around that time, and having a broader driver review system that includes written feedback and star ratings.
