# Exercise 3 Project Risks and Risk Management

## Project Risk Register

The following risk register identifies potential risks that may affect the development and successful completion of the Campus Shuttle Reservation App. Team member availability and integration problems are considered the two major project risks because they could directly affect the completion and combination of project components. However, these risks can be contained through early planning, regular communication, consistent development practices, and frequent integration testing.

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Possible Cause | Probability | Impact | Mitigation/Response Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | The project requirements may change or remain unclear during development. | Team members or stakeholders may have different expectations regarding reservation rules, shuttle timetables, notifications, live location tracking, and no-show procedures. | Low | Medium | The team will document and review the requirements before implementation. Proposed changes will be discussed and approved before being added. Since the app has a clearly defined scope, this risk can be easily contained through regular requirement reviews. |
| R2 | The team may experience difficulty implementing some technical features because it is unfamiliar with some of the required technologies. | Features such as authentication, real-time device location tracking, notifications, and database integration may require technologies that the team has not previously used. | Medium | Medium | Although the team is unfamiliar with some technologies, learning resources, documentation, tutorials, and instructor guidance are accessible. The team will research the technologies early, create small prototypes, and divide tasks according to each member's skills. |
| R3 | A team member may be unavailable or unable to complete an assigned task on time. | Examinations, conflicting deadlines, illness, personal commitments, or an uneven distribution of work may affect team member availability. | High | High | Tasks will be assigned early and tracked through GitHub. Team members will provide regular progress updates and document their work so another member can continue it if necessary. Tasks may also be redistributed when a member becomes unavailable. With regular monitoring and redistribution, this risk can be contained. |
| R4 | Integration problems may occur when combining work completed by different team members. | Team members may modify the same files, use inconsistent naming conventions, or develop components that do not interact correctly. | High | High | The team will agree on the app structure, database design, interfaces, and naming conventions before development. Members will use separate branches, commit frequently, review changes before merging, and perform integration testing after every increment. Frequent testing will allow integration problems to be identified early and contained. |
| R5 | The project may fall behind schedule. | The team may underestimate the time required for a task or encounter unexpected technical problems. | Low | Medium | The project will be divided into smaller tasks with internal deadlines. The team will monitor progress weekly, complete essential features first, and reserve time for testing and corrections. This makes minor schedule delays easier to contain. |
| R6 | User information or reservation data may be accessed or modified without authorization. | Weak authentication, insufficient input validation, insecure data storage, or programming errors may create security and privacy problems. | Low | High | The app will require authentication, validate user input, restrict functions according to user roles, and avoid storing unnecessary personal information. Important security controls will also be tested before deployment. |
| R7 | Shuttle timetable, location, or seat-availability information may become inaccurate. | Delayed database updates, simultaneous reservations, cancellations, device-location errors, or outdated timetable information may result in incorrect data. | Low | High | The app will update seat availability immediately after bookings and cancellations, prevent reservations beyond shuttle capacity, and obtain the shuttle's live location automatically from the driver's device. The transport administrator will update the shuttle timetable, while drivers will only report delays, lateness, and no-shows. |
| R8 | Students, drivers, or the transport administrator may be reluctant to adopt the app. | Users may be unfamiliar with the app or may consider reporting delays and no-shows an additional responsibility. | Low | Medium | The app will use a simple interface and require only limited manual input. Live location will be obtained automatically from the driver's device, while drivers will only report delays, lateness, and no-shows. The app's accountability, reliable timetable information, and notifications will provide clear benefits that encourage adoption. |

## Risk Monitoring

The team will review the risk register regularly throughout the project. Team member availability and integration problems will receive the highest priority because they are the two major risks. The probability and impact of each risk will be updated when project conditions change. The proposed mitigation strategies will help the team identify problems early and contain them before they significantly affect the project.
